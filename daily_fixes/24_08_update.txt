Prescription, Nurse Billing & Optical Prescription Enhancements
Priority: HIGH PRIORITY
1. Doctor Prescription – Post-Prescription Editing
•	Enable doctors to edit the prescription even after the prescription has been generated/saved.
•	The doctor should be able to modify medicines, dosage, frequency, duration, instructions, and other prescription details as required.
•	The latest edited prescription should be reflected in the patient record and available for printing/downloading.
•	Maintain an audit trail of prescription edits, including the doctor, date, and time of modification.
2. Consultation Fee Collection – Nurse Login
•	Enable consultation fee collection functionality in the Nurse Login.
•	Nurses should be able to collect and record the consultation fee on behalf of the hospital.
•	Provide appropriate payment modes such as Cash, UPI, Card, and other configured payment methods.
•	The payment status should be updated automatically in the patient's billing/consultation record.
•	The system should capture payment details, amount, date/time, and the staff member who collected the payment.
3. Optical Prescription – Machine Prescribed vs Doctor Prescribed
•	Split the Optical Prescription into two clearly identifiable sections:
•	• Machine Prescribed – values/results generated or recommended based on the optical/measurement machine.
•	• Doctor Prescribed – the final prescription values entered or approved by the doctor.
•	Both sections should be stored separately in the patient record.
•	The doctor-prescribed values should be clearly identified as the final clinical prescription.
•	Both machine and doctor prescription details should be available for viewing, printing, and future reference.

Implementation Priority: HIGH
These changes are required to improve prescription workflow flexibility, streamline consultation fee collection, and clearly distinguish machine-generated optical measurements from the doctor's final prescription.
4. Prescription Page – Diagnosis & Medicines Placement
•	Rearrange the prescription sections so that Diagnosis and Medicines appear immediately below the Laboratory Tests section and above the Advice section.
•	The revised order should be: Patient Details → Clinical Notes → Optical Notes → Eye Examination → Laboratory Tests → Diagnosis → Medicines → Advice → Follow-up.
•	Ensure the updated sequence is reflected consistently in the prescription display, print, and download formats.
